Your first session

After {{ params.studentName }}’s first session, one of our team will be in touch to check in and make sure it was a great fit. If you’re happy to continue — fantastic, and payment for the first session will be processed within 24 hours.

Your Weekly Plan

Your Weekly Plan includes a recurring session each week at the confirmed time. To make sure {{ params.studentName }} gets the most out of tutoring, and to make the matching process worthwhile for everyone — we have a minimum commitment period of five sessions before any changes to your plan can be made.

We know life happens, so here’s how we handle it:

During your first five sessions, one reschedule is available if you need it. The session needs to be moved to another time within the same week, subject to availability. Beyond that, sessions that can’t go ahead will be charged and a makeup credit will be issued for you to use later.
Once you’re through the first five sessions, you’ll have a little more flexibility — up to two reschedules or cancellations per school term, at no charge, with at least 24 hours notice. After that, the same makeup credit policy applies.
Makeup credits are valid for ten weeks and you can hold up to two at a time.
Session no-shows are charged in full, with no credit issued. If something comes up, just let us know as soon as you can.
When school holidays come around, we’ll get in touch with you to confirm if you’d like to continue through the holidays or pause.

You can read the full Terms of Service at any time on our website or in your portal account.

Getting in touch

All scheduling changes and cancellations need to come through us, rather than directly to your tutor. The easiest way is to reply to any of our emails, text/call 0485 883 221, or submit a request through your portal account. We aim to get back to you within 1 business day.

What’s next

You’ll receive your portal invite shortly. When you log in for the first time, you’ll be prompted to add a payment method to complete your account setup. No payment is taken at this stage, and your first session is only charged after we’ve checked in with you following that session.

If you have any questions, we’re always happy to help.
